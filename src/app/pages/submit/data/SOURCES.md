# Data sources

- `countries.json`: English country and territory names from the `Intl.DisplayNames` region list (current ISO 3166-1 codes plus Kosovo; deprecated and non-country codes removed).
- `cities.json`: per country, the capital plus the most populous cities (up to 20, alphabetical), derived from GeoNames `cities15000` (https://www.geonames.org/), licensed under CC BY 4.0. Country keys match `countries.json`.
