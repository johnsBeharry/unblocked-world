Submit to the Poster Contest

Use this page to submit poster work responding to the theme: Ownership. Before submitting, review the process and guidelines below.

Submission Process

  1. Review the Ownership theme.
  2. Create one or more original poster responses.
  3. Prepare your files.
  4. Join UNBLOCKED and confirm your email with a code we send you. No password needed.
  5. Add your posters. Everything saves as you go.
  6. Confirm the submission terms and submit.
  7. Your posters will then be reviewed by the international jury.

Rules and Eligibility

  • Open to individual and collaborative entries.
  • You can submit more than one poster, on the same day or later.
  • Submitted work must be original.
  • Accepted file types: JPG, PNG, PDF, GIF or MP4.
  • Static posters (JPG, PNG, PDF) must be under 5 MB.
  • Motion posters (GIF, MP4) must be under 20 MB.
  • Up to 3 supporting files per poster.
  • Use the file name format: YourName_PosterTitle.
  • Selected work may require additional formats later.
